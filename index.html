About the Studio

Planning that feels personal, not packaged.

The Aisle Studio is a boutique wedding planning and styling company for personalized planning and couples who want a beautiful day without losing the feeling and intention behind it.

We keep the planning process organized, collaborative, and centered on what matters most to you, creating celebrations that feel thoughtful, not formulaic.

Services

Choose the kind of support you need.

Three straightforward ways to work together. From the first decisions to the final details, you get to choose the level of planning and support that fits where you are in the process.

Full Planning

For couples who want a stress-free planning experience and a wedding day that feels like them. We handle all the details, from start to finish. Guiding you through every step of the process. We manage the planning process while keeping all the choices aligned with your vision.

Includes:

  • Full planning and coordination
  • Vendor management and negotiations
  • Design and styling directions
  • Day-of coordination and management

Partial Planning

For the couples who already have plans but want some support and still want to be involved in the planning process. We work with you to create a plan that fits your needs and budget. We step in where you need it most.

Includes:

  • Planning and progress reviews
  • Vendor recommendations and introductions
  • Timeline creation and execution
  • Final planning guidance

Wedding Day Coordination

For the couples who are in the final stages of planning and want to ensure everything is perfect on the big day. We provide a comprehensive review and final touches to make sure your wedding day is exactly as you envision. Between timeline, vendors, and final logistics, let us handle the details so you can focus on enjoying your special day.

Includes:

  • Final planning and day-of coordination
  • Vendor finalization and confirmations
  • Detailed day-of timeline
  • Day-of coordination
